# Liquibase Secure 6.0 documentation

Everything you need to run change tracking, policy governance and automated delivery in one platform. Start with the capability you came for, or follow the lifecycle from an empty project to a governed pipeline.

- [Get started](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/intro-to-liquibase-6-0-2)
- [What's new in 6.0](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/release-notes-6-0-2/6-0-0-secure-release-notes)

## Choose your capabilities

What each capability does, and where its documentation starts.

### Change Intelligence

What Liquibase records. Every operation that runs against a registered database, the changesets it carried, the policy results it produced, and the drift it revealed.

- [What is Change Intelligence?](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/choose-your-capability-6-0-2/what-is-change-intelligence)
- [Monitor deployment activity](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/monitor-6-0-2/monitor-overview)
- [Review policy check results](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/monitor-6-0-2/what-are-policy-check-results-in-the-ui)
- [Review drift detection](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/monitor-6-0-2/what-is-drift-detection)

### Change Governance

How governance behaves. The policy model, how assignments resolve, what enforcement does, and what gets recorded when a change is blocked.

- [What is Change Governance?](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/choose-your-capability-6-0-2/what-is-change-governance)
- [Browse the policy catalog](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/governance-6-0-2/browse-the-policy-catalog)
- [Define policy assignments](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/governance-6-0-2/assign-policy-checks-to-your-assets)
- [Configure enforcement](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/governance-6-0-2/set-policy-check-severity-and-exit-codes)

### Change Automation

The runner. How changes are validated, deployed and rolled back from the command line and from your pipeline, and how they report back.

- [What is Change Automation?](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/choose-your-capability-6-0-2/what-is-change-automation)
- [Set up Change Automation](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/create-6-0-2/set-up-secure-automation)
- [Automate with flow files](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/deliver-changes-6-0-2/what-is-a-liquibase-flow)
- [Execute changes through CI/CD](https://docs.liquibase.com/secure/deliver-changes-6-0-2/design-your-deployment-pipeline)
